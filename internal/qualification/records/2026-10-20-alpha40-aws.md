state stayed the same. A promotion-eligible AWS row needs either a supported resumed-attempt model
or one clean single-attempt run.

## Why the Kafka observation stopped

`internal/qualification/kafka-delivery.py` compares the broker authority with the delivered
consumer credential. That requires reading the broker Secret (`redpanda-users`), the broker CA
(`redpanda-default-cert`) and the consumer credential Secret in the application namespace. The
qualifier is denied `secrets` by design, and the deploy identity is not granted `redpanda-users` in
the platform namespace (only `redpanda-default-cert`, for projection). The observation therefore
fails closed with `kubectl --kubeconfig failed (exit 1); credential output withheld`.

This is a security-boundary decision, not a proven delivery defect: the workloads are running and
the deploy reports the delivery, but the row cannot read it under the declared authorities.
Resolving it requires an explicit choice — a resource-named read for the qualifier, a value-free
delivery observation (for example, comparing Secret identity and projection rather than the
password), or a separate broker-observation identity — and a corresponding qualification contract
change. It is not repaired by widening the deploy identity in this record.

## Defects fixed by PR #1592 during this campaign

- Bind the qualification transport in the deploy-declared database namespace, read from
  `sol-database-identity:` rather than derived.
- Re-establish the transport on every `app` invocation; a kubeconfig left by an earlier attempt
  does not show that the current observation set is bound.
- Pass the deploy identity's authority as `submission_authority`; the migration receipt is checked
  against the identity that submitted it, not the qualifier that observed it.
- Accept kubectl's generic `List` envelope and its empty collection `resourceVersion`.
- Report a CA phase's actual verdict and member errors instead of `ok: true` on a failed phase.
- Acknowledge the missing-secret probe's own expected consumer-group change.
- Accept an unrelated workload-identity projection while failing closed on a projected CA.
- Observe Kafka delivery through the qualification transport rather than the cluster-access
  identity.

These are qualification-tooling changes; the installed candidate's product behavior is unchanged.
The product defects found in the preceding alpha.39 campaign (#1588 broker CA RBAC, #1590 targeted
not-found) are in alpha.40 and were exercised here.
