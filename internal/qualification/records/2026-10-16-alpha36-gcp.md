## Findings this run produced

No product or provider defect: every phase the row claims completed on its first attempt.

One **harness narration** inaccuracy. The row reports the bootstrap stop as "a fresh durable target
refuses at its required-secret stop until the operator credentials exist", but the deploy actually
refused at the **migration gate** (`app-deploy-bootstrap.log`). The row's behaviour is correct — it
applies migrations next and then deploys — but the message asserts a boundary that did not happen,
which would mislead an operator reading the transcript. Reported here; no issue filed.
