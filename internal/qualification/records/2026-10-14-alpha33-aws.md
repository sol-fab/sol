# AWS 2b acceptance verdict `2b-a33` on `v0.1.0-alpha.33` (2026-10-14)

**Verdict: the AWS row is qualified on `v0.1.0-alpha.33`.** All three claims that this arc exists
to establish were exercised on real infrastructure and passed: `app_verify_database_delivery`,
the application transaction's read-back, and the populated teardown. Delivery **and** consumption
are established — the read-back is what makes the consumption claim.

The candidate was `3b2d3adb` throughout. Two harness defects (#1446, #1447) were fixed between
app passes; those change the rig, not the product, and fixing a stub or an assertion mid-window
is what a harness finding is for. The window's shape is disclosed below so a reader knows exactly
what was run — but the disclosure records *how* the claims were exercised, not whether they
passed. They passed, on this tag.

The earlier `alpha.29`, `alpha.31` and `alpha.32` records are partials for a different reason:
they never reached the claims. This one reached all of them.

This is the **AWS half** of the acceptance. The acceptance is both providers on one tag, so
`v0.1.0-alpha.33` is qualified on AWS and remains unqualified until the GCP verdict lands on the
same revision.

### Delivery **and** consumption, this time

The earlier partials could only report delivery. Here both are established: the class Secrets
exist in the namespaces the migration Job and the consuming units read from, the consumer's own
Secret carries no master copy — **and** a workload ran, produced an effect, and the effect was
read back out of PostgreSQL by the service. That read-back is the consumption claim: the unit is
running against the database the deploy delivered, not against an operator's copy.

## Window shape (disclosed)

The AWS row completed, but the app phase ran **three times** on the one target. The candidate
never changed; what changed between passes was the apparatus and one input from this run:

1. **Pass 1** (cold): proved the migration-gate refusal and `sol migrate apply` in order, then
   the deploy refused the absent operator secrets (expected), the row supplied both scopes, and
   the deploy **succeeded** — #1440's fix, live, on its first production use. The delivery
   assertion then failed reporting the ddl-creds Secret absent, because the assertion listed
   secrets cluster-wide with a namespace-scoped identity and discarded the Forbidden (#1446).
   The class Secrets were present.
2. **Pass 2**: after the #1446 **harness** fix, the app phase re-ran — the deploys now succeed
   and their refusal steps are skipped, so the gate and secret refusals are proven by pass 1
   rather than re-proven here — reached the delivery assertion (**PASS**), then failed in
   `app_populate_sol_repositories` on a host-local image tag the publish path need not create
   (#1447), again a **harness** defect.
3. **Pass 3**: after the #1447 harness fix, the app phase reached and passed populate, transport
   separation, and the transaction.

One operator error was found and corrected between passes: `QUALIFIER_ROLE` was set to a role ARN
while the row takes the role *name*. The harness's own error message named the invalid role name;
no finding was filed, because the fault was in this run's environment rather than in Sol or the
harness.

So the refusals were proven on the cold pass and the completing assertions on a resumed pass.
Both are the same candidate, the same tag and the same target. **The verdict rests on the claims
having been exercised and passed on `3b2d3adb`; it does not rest on their having been exercised in
one uninterrupted process.** A reader who needs a single cold run to be satisfied should treat
that as outstanding, and the record says which assertions came from which pass.

## Teardown

Destroy failed twice more at `terraform init` on an expired SSO session — the operational risk
filed as **#1443** during the alpha.32 attempt, which recurred here and left the substrate live
(EKS, RDS, 4 nodes, VPC/NAT/EIP, 10 volumes) until re-authentication. After re-auth: `destroy
returned success`, and the independent inventory read ABSENT for every class. This is the second
occurrence of that failure mode, and it remains unfixed by design (filed, not patched).
