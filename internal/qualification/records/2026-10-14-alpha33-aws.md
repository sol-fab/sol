### Delivery **and** consumption, this time

The earlier partials could only report delivery. Here both are established: the class Secrets
exist in the namespaces the migration Job and the consuming units read from, the consumer's own
Secret carries no master copy — **and** a workload ran, produced an effect, and the effect was
read back out of PostgreSQL by the service. That read-back is the consumption claim: the unit is
running against the database the deploy delivered, not against an operator's copy.

## Window shape (disclosed)

The AWS row completed, but the app phase ran **three times** on the one target, with two harness
fixes and one operator-input correction between passes:

1. **Pass 1** (cold): proved the migration-gate refusal and `sol migrate apply` in order, then
   the deploy refused the absent operator secrets (expected), the row supplied both scopes, and
   the deploy **succeeded** — #1440's fix, live, on its first production use. The delivery
   assertion then failed reporting the ddl-creds Secret absent, because the assertion listed
   secrets cluster-wide with a namespace-scoped identity and discarded the Forbidden (#1446).
   The class Secrets were present.
2. **Pass 2**: after the #1446 fix, the app phase re-ran (the deploys now succeed and their
   refusal steps are skipped, so the gate/secret refusals are proven by pass 1, not re-proven
   here), reached the delivery assertion — **PASS** — then failed in `app_populate_sol_repositories`
   on a host-local image tag the publish path need not create (#1447).
3. **Pass 3**: after the #1447 fix, the app phase reached and passed populate, transport
   separation, and the transaction. One operator error was found and corrected between passes:
   `QUALIFIER_ROLE` was set to a role ARN while the row takes the role *name* — the harness's own
   error message named the invalid role name, and no finding was filed because the fault was in
   the run's environment, not in Sol or the harness.

So this is **not a single uninterrupted cold pass**. The refusals were proven on the cold pass
and the completing assertions on a resumed pass, on the same tag and the same target. A reader
who needs a single-pass cold run should treat that as still outstanding.
